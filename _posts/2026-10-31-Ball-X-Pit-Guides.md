Hello guys, long time no see. Today I wanted to share my excitement about the game called Ball x Pit. Basically the game is roguelike bullet hell. It's kinda fun if you wanted to have a new perspective and previously you love to play Vampire Survivors or Brotato. Enjoy the guide and don't forget to check out [Here](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2062430/BALL_x_PIT/)

## Overview & Ball Tier Classification

The guide uses a custom four-tier classification system based on fusion requirements:

- **Tier 0:** Basic balls *(21 total: Bleed through Flesh)*.
- **Tier 1:** A combination of **2 "Tier 0" (Basic) balls** *(plus **Elemental**, which requires 4 specific Tier 0 balls)*.
- **Tier 2:** A combination of **1 "Tier 1" ball** and **1 specific "Tier 0" ball**.
- **Tier 3:** A combination of **2 (or 3) specific "Tier 1" balls**.

## Author Notes & Community Tips

- **New Update Indicators (`★`):** Star markers on cards in the infographic highlight balls and passives introduced in **The Naturalist Update**.
- **Swamp Formula:** Confirmed as **Poison + Earthquake = Swamp** (added in the August 22 update).
- **Unlocking the Charm Ball:** Unlocks in the **HEAVENLYxGATES** stage, or alternatively by unlocking the **Carouser** character via building the **Party House** (using the blueprint obtained in the **VASTxVoid** stage).
